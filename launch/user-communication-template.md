# User Communication Template

Adapt and send before or on the day of launch.

---

## Template 1 — Announcement Email / Teams Post

**Subject:** Introducing [Agent Name] — your new AI assistant for [topic]

---

Hi team,

We're launching **[Agent Name]**, an AI assistant designed to help you with **[1-sentence description of what it does]**.

**What it can help you with:**
- [Topic 1 — e.g., "Checking your annual leave balance"]
- [Topic 2 — e.g., "Submitting leave requests"]
- [Topic 3 — e.g., "Finding HR policies and procedures"]

**What it cannot help with:**
- [Out-of-scope topic 1 — e.g., "Payroll queries → contact payroll@company.com"]
- [Out-of-scope topic 2 — e.g., "IT support → contact the IT helpdesk"]

**How to use it:**
- **In Microsoft Teams:** Search for **[Agent Name]** in the Apps section, or click the link below: [Teams deep link]
- **In Microsoft Copilot (if enabled):** Open Copilot and ask it to talk to [Agent Name]
- **On our website (if applicable):** Visit [URL] and click the chat icon

**Important:** [Agent Name] is an AI assistant — it may occasionally make mistakes. If it gives you an answer you're not sure about, please verify with [contact/source].

**Feedback:** If something doesn't work as expected, or you have suggestions, please reply to this message or contact [feedback contact].

Thanks,
[Sender name]
