# Launch

Three documents covering the final step before users see your agent.

---

## What to complete and in what order

| File | What it covers | Complete when |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| [`01-launch-checklist.md`](01-launch-checklist.md) | Tick-box gate — every item must pass before go-live | Day before launch |
| [`02-user-communication-template.md`](02-user-communication-template.md) | Email/Teams announcement to notify users | Day of launch |
| [`03-hypercare-guide.md`](03-hypercare-guide.md) | First 2 weeks post-launch — who watches what, how to respond | After launch |

---

## Sequence

1. **`01-launch-checklist.md`** — Gate: every item ticked and signed off before go-live
2. **`02-user-communication-template.md`** — Send to users 5–7 days before launch, then again on launch day
3. **`03-hypercare-guide.md`** — Follow for the first 14 days post-launch

> After hypercare ends, hand off to steady-state monitoring: [`../operations/README.md`](../operations/README.md).

---

## Minimum before any user touches the agent

- [ ] `01-launch-checklist.md` — all items checked, business owner signed
- [ ] Governance docs complete — `../governance/02-ai-ethics-checklist.md` + `03-security-review.md`
- [ ] At least one UAT sign-off round complete — `../project-delivery/13-uat-test-plan.md`

→ Post-launch operations: [`../operations/README.md`](../operations/README.md)
